## 1. Mission Research Sources

All mission-related facts, historical information, scientific details, and technical references in this project are based on official NASA publications and resources.

### 1.1 Opportunity Rover (Mars Exploration Rover-B)

- [NASA Science — Opportunity](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/mer-opportunity/)  
  Official mission overview, landing information, scientific objectives, mission timeline, and total distance travelled.

- [NASA Science — Mars Exploration Rovers: Spirit and Opportunity](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/mars-exploration-rovers-spirit-and-opportunity/)  
  Background on the Mars Exploration Rover mission, its scientific objectives, and the discoveries made by both rovers.

- [NASA — NASA's Record-Setting Opportunity Rover Mission on Mars Comes to an End](https://www.nasa.gov/news-release/nasas-record-setting-opportunity-rover-mission-on-mars-comes-to-end/)  
  Official announcement of the mission's conclusion on February 13, 2019, including its final communication and mission achievements.

- [NASA JPL — Six Things to Know About NASA's Opportunity Mars Rover](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/news/six-things-to-know-about-nasas-opportunity-mars-rover/)  
  Additional information about Opportunity's mission history, scientific contributions, and engineering achievements.

### 1.2 Lunar Roving Vehicle (Apollo 15, Apollo 16 & Apollo 17)

- [NASA NSSDC — The Apollo Lunar Roving Vehicle](https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/lunar/apollo_lrv.html)  
  Official historical and technical information about the Lunar Roving Vehicle, including its design, deployment, specifications, and recorded travel distances.

- [NASA NSSDC — Apollo 15 Mission Record](https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/nmc/spacecraft/display.action?id=1971-063C)  
  Historical mission record for Apollo 15, the first Apollo mission to deploy and operate a Lunar Roving Vehicle.

- [NASA — Apollo 17 Press Kit](https://science.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/Apollo_17.pdf)  
  Official mission documentation containing information about the Lunar Roving Vehicle and its use during Apollo 17.

**Historical clarification:**

The Lunar Roving Vehicle was deployed during Apollo 15, Apollo 16, and Apollo 17. Apollo 12 is not included as an LRV deployment mission.

According to NASA NSSDC, the recorded LRV travel distances were:

- Apollo 15: 27.8 km
- Apollo 16: 26.7 km
- Apollo 17: 35.9 km

Combined distance: approximately 90.4 km.

### 1.3 Ingenuity Mars Helicopter

- [NASA — After Three Years on Mars, NASA's Ingenuity Helicopter Mission Ends](https://www.nasa.gov/news-release/after-three-years-on-mars-nasas-ingenuity-helicopter-mission-ends/)  
  Official announcement of the mission's conclusion on January 25, 2024.

- [NASA Science — Ingenuity's 72 Flights](https://science.nasa.gov/resource/ingenuity-72-pathbreaking-flights-poster/)

## 4. Research Verification Notes

The following factual details are cross-referenced with the official NASA sources listed above.

### 4.1 Opportunity Rover

- Opportunity landed on Mars on January 24, 2004.
- Its last communication with Earth was received on June 10, 2018.
- NASA declared the mission complete on February 13, 2019.
- The rover travelled approximately 45.16 km during its mission.

### 4.2 Lunar Roving Vehicle

- The LRV was used during Apollo 15, Apollo 16, and Apollo 17.
- It enabled astronauts to travel farther across the lunar surface during their exploration activities.
- NASA NSSDC provides the historical vehicle information and mission travel distances.
- Apollo 12 is not identified as an LRV deployment mission.

### 4.3 Ingenuity Mars Helicopter

- Ingenuity completed its first powered, controlled flight on Mars on April 19, 2021.
- Its final flight took place on January 18, 2024.
- The helicopter completed 72 flights.
- NASA announced the end of its mission on January 25, 2024.
